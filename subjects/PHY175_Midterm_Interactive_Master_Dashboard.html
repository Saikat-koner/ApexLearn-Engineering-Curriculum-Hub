📚 Unit 1: Solid State Physics & Semiconductor Physics

1. Free Electron Theory

  • Classical (Drude-Lorentz, 1900): Treats valence electrons as classical ideal gas. Conductivity \(\sigma = \frac{n e^2 \tau}{m}\), Drift velocity \(v_d = \mu E\).
  • Wiedemann-Franz Law: \(\frac{K}{\sigma T} = \text{Lorentz Number } L = 2.44 \times 10^{-8}\text{ W}\Omega\text{K}^{-2}\).
  • Failures: Electronic heat capacity (\(\frac{3}{2}R\) vs actual \(\approx 0.01R\)), \(\rho \propto T\) (classical predicted \(\sqrt{T}\)), failed to explain semiconductors.

2. Drift vs Diffusion Current

  • Drift: Driven by electric field \(E\). \(J_{\text{drift}} = q(n\mu_n + p\mu_p)E\).
  • Diffusion: Driven by concentration gradient. \(J_{n,\text{diff}} = q D_n \frac{dn}{dx}\), \(J_{p,\text{diff}} = -q D_p \frac{dp}{dx}\).
  • Einstein Relation: \(\frac{D_n}{\mu_n} = \frac{D_p}{\mu_p} = \frac{k_B T}{q} = V_T \approx 26\text{ mV}\) at \(300\text{ K}\).

3. Fermi Level & Fermi-Dirac Distribution

  • \(f(E) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{(E - E_F)/k_B T}}\). At \(T > 0\text{ K}\), \(f(E_F) = 0.5\) (50% occupation probability).
  • Intrinsic: \(E_F\) is at the middle of bandgap \(\frac{E_c + E_v}{2}\).
  • N-type: \(E_F\) shifts up towards \(E_c\). P-type: \(E_F\) shifts down towards \(E_v\).

4. Hall Effect (Top Exam Topic!)

  • Transverse voltage \(V_H = \frac{R_H B I}{t}\) across current-carrying sample in perpendicular magnetic field \(B\).
  • Hall Coefficient \(R_H = -\frac{1}{ne}\) for N-type (\(R_H < 0\)), \(R_H = +\frac{1}{pe}\) for P-type (\(R_H > 0\)).
  • Carrier concentration: \(n = \frac{1}{e |R_H|}\). Mobility: \(\mu = \sigma |R_H|\).

⚡ Unit 2: Electrical Laws, Devices & Communications

1. Kirchhoff's Laws

  • KCL: \(\sum I = 0\) (Conservation of Electric Charge).
  • KVL: \(\sum V = 0\) (Conservation of Energy).

2. PN Diode & Breakdown

  • Barrier potential: \(0.7\text{ V}\) for Silicon, \(0.3\text{ V}\) for Germanium.
  • Zener Breakdown: Heavy doping, thin depletion (\(<10\text{ nm}\)), direct bond rupture, \(V_Z < 6\text{ V}\), negative temp coeff.
  • Avalanche Breakdown: Light doping, wide depletion, impact ionization / carrier multiplication, \(V_A > 6\text{ V}\), positive temp coeff.

3. BJT & CMOS

  • Doping order: Emitter > Collector > Base. Size order: Collector > Emitter > Base.
  • Active region (Amplifier): EB forward, CB reverse. \(\beta = \frac{\alpha}{1-\alpha}\), \(\alpha = \frac{\beta}{1+\beta}\).
  • CMOS Inverter: PMOS pull-up to \(V_{DD}\), NMOS pull-down to GND. Zero static power consumption.

4. Solar Cell & Optical Fiber

  • Solar Cell: Photovoltaic effect. \(FF = \frac{V_{mp} I_{mp}}{V_{oc} I_{sc}}\), \(\eta = \frac{V_{oc} I_{sc} FF}{P_{in}} \times 100\%\).
  • Optical Fiber: Total Internal Reflection (\(n_1 > n_2\)). \(NA = \sin\theta_a = \sqrt{n_1^2 - n_2^2}\). Single mode when \(V \le 2.405\).

2. Binary Codes & Complements

  • 2's Complement: 1's comp + 1. (8-bit signed range: \(-128\) to \(+127\)).
  • Excess-3: \(\text{BCD} + 0011_2\). Self-complementing code.
  • Gray Code: Unit-distance code (\(1\) bit changes per transition). \(G_i = B_{i+1} \oplus B_i\).
  • Boolean Absorption: \(A + A'B = A + B\), \(A + BC = (A+B)(A+C)\).

📐 Quick Formula Cheatsheet for 30/30 Marks

1. Drift Velocity: v_d = μ * E = (e * τ / m) * E
2. Conductivity: σ = n * e * μ_n + p * e * μ_p = 1 / ρ
3. Mass Action Law: n * p = n_i^2
4. Hall Voltage: V_H = (R_H * B * I) / t
5. Hall Coefficient: R_H = -1/(n*e) [N-type] | R_H = +1/(p*e) [P-type]
6. BJT Current Gain: β = α / (1 - α) | α = β / (1 + β) | I_E = I_B + I_C
7. Solar Cell Fill Factor: FF = (V_mp * I_mp) / (V_oc * I_sc)
8. Optical Fiber NA: NA = sin(θ_a) = √(n1² - n2²) ≈ n1 * √(2Δ)
9. V-Number: V = (2π * a / λ) * NA [Single Mode: V ≤ 2.405]
10. 2's Complement 8-Bit Range: -2^(n-1) to +(2^(n-1) - 1) = -128 to +127
