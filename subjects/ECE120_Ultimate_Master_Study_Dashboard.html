🧰 Summary of All 6 Laboratory Practicals & Precautions

⚠️ Crucial Laboratory Precautions:
  • DMM Ammeter Hazard: Never measure voltage with the DMM set to Current mode (causes dead short circuit and blows fuse).
  • Diode Current Limiter: Always insert a 1kΩ resistor in series with a diode in forward bias to prevent thermal runaway.
  • Active-LOW IR Sensor: Code condition must be if (digitalRead(pin) == LOW) on obstacle detection.
  • TTL Voltage Limit: Never apply > 5.5V DC to TTL 74xx ICs.

🎯 Top 10 High-Frequency Master Viva Voce Q&A

1. Why are NAND and NOR called Universal Gates?
Because any Boolean logic function (AND, OR, NOT, XOR, XNOR) can be implemented solely using combinations of NAND or NOR gates.
2. What is the Knee / Cut-in Voltage of Silicon vs Germanium Diodes?
Silicon diode cut-in voltage is ~0.7V; Germanium diode cut-in voltage is ~0.3V.
3. State Kirchhoff's Laws and their conservation principles.
KVL (∑V = 0) is based on Conservation of Energy; KCL (∑I = 0) is based on Conservation of Electric Charge.
4. Explain the difference between a Half Adder and a Full Adder.
A Half Adder adds 2 bits (A, B); a Full Adder adds 3 bits (A, B, and Carry-in from previous stage).
5. How does an IR Sensor module work and why is it Active-LOW?
An IR LED emits 940nm infrared light. When an obstacle reflects light to the photodiode receiver, an on-board LM393 comparator pulls the output pin to Logic LOW (0V).
6. What is the Maximum Power Transfer Theorem condition and efficiency?
Max power is transferred when load resistance equals Thevenin resistance (RL = Rth). Maximum efficiency is 50%.
7. What are the standard VCC and GND pins for a 14-pin digital IC?
Pin 14 is VCC (+5V) and Pin 7 is Ground (0V).
8. What is the IC 7402 pinout exception?
In IC 7402 NOR gate, Pin 1 is Output, while Pins 2 & 3 are Inputs (opposite of standard 7400/7408 ICs).
9. What is Dynamic Resistance in a Diode?
rd = ΔVD / ΔID ≈ 26mV / ID, calculated as the reciprocal of the forward V-I characteristic curve slope.
10. What is the maximum current an Arduino GPIO pin can supply?
40 mA absolute maximum (safe continuous operating current is ≤ 20 mA).
