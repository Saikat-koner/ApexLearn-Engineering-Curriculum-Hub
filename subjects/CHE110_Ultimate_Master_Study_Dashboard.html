📖 Standard Reference Textbooks with Deep Chapter Context

Perspectives in Environmental Studies (7th Ed) — Anubha Kaushik & C. P. Kaushik (New Age)
Ch 1 & 2 (Environment & Sustainability): 4 Spheres (Atmosphere, Hydrosphere, Lithosphere, Biosphere), Multidisciplinary scope, Brundtland Commission 1987, 3 Pillars of Sustainability, Carrying Capacity, and 17 UN SDGs.
Ch 3 (Natural Resources): Groundwater over-extraction, Deforestation, Mining conflicts, Dams vs tribal displacement, Renewable vs non-renewable energy transitions.
Ch 4 (Ecosystem Dynamics): Biotic/Abiotic structure, 10% Lindeman's Rule, Food chains/webs, Ecological Pyramids (Energy is always upright, inverted aquatic biomass), Ecological Succession (Hydrosere vs Xerosere).
🎯 Exam Focus: Draw Ecosystem Energy Flow (10% rule), 3 Pillars Venn diagram, and explain Primary vs Secondary succession stages.
Textbook of Environmental Studies for Undergraduate Courses (3rd Ed) — Erach Bharucha (UGC / Orient BlackSwan)
Ch 4 (Biodiversity & Conservation): 3 Levels (Genetic, Species, Ecosystem), 4 Ecosystem Services, Norman Myers Hotspot criteria (>=1500 endemic plants, >=70% habitat loss), 4 Indian Hotspots (Himalaya, Western Ghats, Indo-Burma, Sundaland), HIPPO Threats, IUCN Red List, In-situ (National Parks, Sanctuaries, Biosphere Reserves, Sacred Groves) vs Ex-situ (Botanical Gardens, Seed Banks, Cryopreservation).
🎯 Exam Focus: Draw In-situ vs Ex-situ Classification Tree, list Hotspot criteria, and describe 4 Indian Hotspots.
Text Book of Environmental Studies (2nd Ed) — D. Dave & S. S. Katewa (Cengage Learning)
Ch 5 (Pollution & Waste Management): Air Pollution (Primary vs Secondary, Photochemical vs Classical Smog, ESP / Scrubbers), Water Pollution (BOD vs COD math, Eutrophication sequence, Minamata / Itai-Itai diseases), Noise & Radiation, Emerging Pollutants (Microplastics, PFAS, E-waste), Ozone Chapman cycle & Montreal Protocol 1987, Acid Rain, 4Rs Solid Waste Management hierarchy, Bhopal Gas Tragedy (MIC).
🎯 Exam Focus: Draw Eutrophication lifecycle, compare Classical vs Photochemical smog, and explain Ozone catalytic destruction.
